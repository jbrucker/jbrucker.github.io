## Best Free / Low-Cost Online Tools

### 1. Media.io AI Hair Color Try-On

* Lets you upload one photo and automatically generate a **grid of multiple realistic hair color results (e.g., 3×3)** so you can compare shades side-by-side.
* Preserves lighting, skin tone, and background while altering hair color.
* Good for quick experimentation with natural and bold tones. ([Media.io][1])

### 2. NanoFlux AI Hair Color Changer

* Free AI tool that applies hair color changes while maintaining hair texture and structure.
* Offers a selection of presets plus custom colors that you can switch between easily.
* Works for portraits and selfies where the hair is clearly visible. ([NanoFlux][2])

### 3. Somake AI Hair Color Changer

* Free tier available with automated hair detection and a **wide palette of natural and fantasy color options** (from blonde/brunette to pastel and vivid hues).
* Very fast processing and minimal settings makes it practical for rapid iteration. ([Somake AI][3])

### 4. AirBrush AI Hair Color Changer

* Browser-based AI hair color tool with multiple shade options (natural and trendy).
* Very easy to use: upload, select color, preview, download. Works on any device. ([AirBrush][4])

### 5. Instasize Hair Color Filter

* Offers a selection of common realistic hair colors (blonde, brunette, black, auburn, platinum, etc.) with AI blending that preserves texture.
* Quick workflow and good for comparison of several classic shades. ([Instasize][5])

### 6. BeautyPlus / iFoto / Fotor Virtual Try-Ons

* These are broader online photo editing suites that include **AI hair color try-on tools**.
* They allow experimenting with various shades and sometimes highlight/ombre effects.
* Some features may require free accounts or limited trial periods. ([BeautyPlus][6])

### How to Get Consistent Results

- **Reproducibility:** Upload the *same photo under identical lighting* and choose the same preset/color each time. AI tools tend to produce very similar outputs when these conditions are matched.

- **Multiple Colors:** Tools that support grid views (like Media.io) or let you save multiple versions make it easier to compare several shades at once.

- **Photo Quality:** For best realism, use a high-resolution portrait with clearly visible hair and neutral lighting.
